I am a software engineering leader who turns complex product requirements into reliable, scalable systems. With 14 years of experience, I work across backend architecture, cloud platforms, APIs, data, and the engineering practices that help teams deliver with confidence.

I have contributed to high-traffic e-commerce, healthcare platforms, and internal tools, leading teams through architecture decisions, platform improvements, and product delivery. My approach is practical: understand the problem, simplify where possible, measure what matters, and build foundations that remain dependable as the product and team grow.

Alongside delivery, I care deeply about people and engineering culture. I mentor engineers, strengthen code-review and collaboration practices, and help teams turn ambitious goals into clear, achievable plans. I believe great software comes from strong systems, thoughtful communication, and teams that are trusted to do their best work.

  • Reliability first

    SLOs, graceful degradation and error budgets — not heroics.

  • Measured, not guessed

    Profilers and traces decide the optimisation, not opinions.

  • Clean interfaces

    Contracts and boundaries that let teams move without collisions.

  • People scale too

    Documentation, review culture and mentoring as part of delivery.
